**Hard constraint:** Junction asked for a video "not a second longer than 3 minutes, and we will be very strict" in its latest published guidance (2024 participant guide, [Project Submission](https://junctionhq.notion.site/Project-Submission-fff59a11813781a38121ccc1a3fb14d4)). Final submission is Sun 15 Nov 10:00. Aim for 2:45.

Voice: plain, confident, a little playful, short sentences, one joke per beat at most. (Style taken from Szymon's own Notion write-ups: first person, story first, self-deprecating about the build, then a straight explanation. See note at the bottom.)

Rules from `PITCH.md` still apply: no "first ever", no real earnings or users, say "scored by our algorithm, explained by Gemini", say which footage is live and which is the simulator. Never use the line "clinically proven".

Format: 16:9, 1080p, burned-in captions (judges watch muted at tables), 48 kHz audio, voice-over recorded on a quiet phone mic held close. Screen capture at 60 fps, glove footage on a clean table with side light.

## Live judging talk track

Context: peer review is face to face at your table (top 5), then 5 pitches to a grand jury of 7 ([2026 site](https://2026.hackjunction.com/)). The jury's stated tastes: a real user problem, something people come back to, speed and pragmatism, a path to production, and a team that can explain what fails ([same page](https://2026.hackjunction.com/)). The DeepMind judge looks for "one unique insight".

### 90-second booth version

Have one person gloved and training already when the judge arrives. Start talking while they are mid-move.

| Time | Say | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "Watch the ring finger. [wrong finger, string goes red] A camera would call that correct. We know which finger, how far, and how late." | Gloved teammate plays; string turns vermilion |
| 0:15 | "Hand skills like piano, table tennis or physio are taught one person at a time. In a Renaissance workshop you learnt by copying the master's hands. That never scaled. We put the master's hands in a file." | Point at the ghost hand and strings |
| 0:35 | "The expert records once with Rokoko gloves. Anyone trains against that recording. Four modes: marionette, shadow, ghost, side by side. The help fades, then the score decides." | Switch two modes live |
| 0:55 | "The score is our algorithm, per finger, with tolerances. Gemini builds the lesson from a sentence and explains the result in plain words. It never scores." | Show the lesson built earlier and the coach line |

1. **"Right note, wrong finger."**
2. **"The master's hands, in a file."**
3. **"Scored by an algorithm, explained by Gemini."**
4. **"One pair of gloves, two players."**
